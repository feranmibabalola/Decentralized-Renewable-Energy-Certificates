# Decentralized Renewable Energy Certificates (D-REC)

A blockchain-based solution for verifying, issuing, trading, and tracking renewable energy certificates.

## Overview

This project implements a decentralized system for Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) using smart contracts. It provides a transparent and efficient way to validate clean energy production, create tradable certificates, facilitate market transactions, and track compliance with renewable energy mandates.

## Key Components

### Generation Verification Contract
- Validates renewable energy production from authorized sources
- Connects with IoT devices and energy meters to verify generation data
- Implements fraud detection and data validation algorithms
- Creates immutable records of verified energy production

### Certificate Issuance Contract
- Mints digital certificates representing verified renewable energy
- Includes metadata about energy source, location, generation time, and environmental attributes
- Implements ERC-721 or similar token standards for unique certificate identification
- Provides provenance tracking through the certificate lifecycle

### Trading Contract
- Facilitates peer-to-peer trading of energy certificates
- Implements auction and direct sale mechanisms
- Handles escrow, settlement, and transfer of ownership
- Includes price discovery mechanisms and market analytics

### Compliance Tracking Contract
- Monitors certificate ownership for regulatory compliance
- Tracks progress toward renewable energy mandates and targets
- Generates compliance reports for regulators and participants
- Implements validation rules for different jurisdictions and standards
